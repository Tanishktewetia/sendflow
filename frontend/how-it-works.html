How It Works

Complete guide to using SendFlow

1. Create Your Account

Sign up for a free SendFlow account in seconds. No credit card required. Simply provide your name, email, and create a password. Your account is instantly ready to use.

2. Configure Your Email Provider

Choose how you want to send emails:

  • SMTP: Connect your Gmail, Outlook, or any SMTP server. Add your email address, display name, SMTP host, port, and app password.
  • Resend API: Use the Resend service by providing your API key and email address.
  • Other providers: More integrations coming soon!

You can add multiple email accounts and switch between them anytime.

3. Prepare Your Recipient List

Prepare a CSV file with your contact information:

  • Required columns: name, email
  • Optional columns: company, customField1, customField2

Upload your CSV file by dragging and dropping it, or click to select. SendFlow will preview the data before processing.

4. Compose Your Campaign

Create your email campaign with these features:

  • Subject Line: Enter your email subject
  • HTML Body: Write your email content with HTML support
  • Personalization: Use dynamic tags like {{name}}, {{email}}, {{company}}, {{customField1}}, {{customField2}}
  • Live Preview: See how your email will look before sending

Example: "Hello {{name}} at {{company}}! Check out our latest updates." Each recipient will see their personalized message.

5. Select Recipients

Choose who receives your campaign:

  • Send to All: Email all recipients from your uploaded list
  • Custom Range: Specify a range (e.g., recipients 1-100 or 50-200)

This flexibility allows you to test campaigns or target specific audience segments.

6. Launch Your Campaign

Click the "Start Campaign" button to send your emails. SendFlow will:

  • Validate your email configuration
  • Process your recipient list
  • Personalize each email with recipient data
  • Send emails through your configured provider
  • Track delivery status in real-time

7. Monitor Your Campaign

Once launched, you can monitor:

  • Total Emails: Number of recipients receiving the campaign
  • Sent: Successfully delivered emails
  • Failed: Emails that couldn't be delivered
  • Pending: Emails currently being processed
  • Success Rate: Percentage of successful deliveries

8. View Campaign Logs

Access detailed logs for each campaign including:

  • Campaign name and creation date
  • Number of recipients
  • Email provider used
  • Delivery statistics
  • Error details for failed emails

Best Practices

Follow these recommendations for best results:

  • Test First: Send a test campaign to a small group before launching to all recipients
  • Personalize: Use dynamic tags to make emails personal and relevant
  • Clean List: Ensure your recipient list has valid, unique email addresses
  • Monitor Delivery: Check success rates and fix any configuration issues
  • Respect Privacy: Always comply with email regulations and recipient preferences
  • Follow Up: Use campaigns strategically to nurture relationships with your audience

Troubleshooting

Common issues and solutions:

  • Email failed to send: Check your email provider configuration and ensure credentials are correct
  • CSV upload error: Verify your CSV has "name" and "email" columns with valid data
  • Personalization not working: Ensure you're using correct tag format {{columnName}}
  • Low success rate: Check recipient email addresses are valid and not marked as spam
